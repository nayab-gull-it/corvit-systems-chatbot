# Corvit Systems Rawalpindi — Admissions & Application Process

## 1. Admission Policy
Corvit Systems Rawalpindi welcomes university students, graduates, and working professionals from Rawalpindi, Islamabad, and across Pakistan. Admissions operate on a **rolling monthly batch system** across all core tracks.

## 3. General Requirements & Eligibility

### A. Foundational IT Courses (CCNA, Web Dev, SEO, Graphic Design, Python)
- **Eligibility**: Open to students, undergraduates (FAST, NUST, COMSATS, Islamic University, etc.), and career-switchers.
- **Prerequisites**: Basic computer literacy. No advanced programming or calculus required for introductory modules.
- **Documents Required**:
  - 1 copy of CNIC or B-Form
  - 1 Passport-sized photograph
  - Filled Corvit registration form

### B. Advanced Professional Tracks (CCNP, CCIE, CEH, CISSP, AWS Pro, DevSecOps)
- Prior foundational certification (e.g. CCNA for CCNP) or demonstrable enterprise experience.

### C. Government-Sponsored Programs (NAVTTC / PM Kamyab Jawan)
- Pakistani national with valid CNIC or smart B-Form.
- Completion of registration on the official NAVTTC portal selecting Corvit Rawalpindi.
