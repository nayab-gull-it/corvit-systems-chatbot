## 3. 100% Free Government-Sponsored Programs (Rawalpindi / Islamabad)
Corvit Systems is an official partner for premier federal skill-building initiatives. For eligible Pakistani citizens residing in the Twin Cities (Rawalpindi and Islamabad), these programs provide **zero-tuition** training:

### A. NAVTTC Free Courses (Rawalpindi & Islamabad Batches)
- **Tuition**: 100% Free (Government Funded).
- **Available Tracks**:
  - Artificial Intelligence (Machine Learning / Deep Learning)
  - Cyber Security & Ethical Hacking
  - Cloud Computing & DevOps
  - Full Stack Web Development
  - NAVTTC Summer of Code
- **Benefits**: Free tuition, hands-on enterprise lab training, free assessment/certification, and government-approved stipends where applicable.
- **Eligibility**: Pakistani nationals with valid CNIC/B-Form meeting NAVTTC selection criteria.

### B. Prime Minister Youth Skill Development Program (PM-Kamyab Jawan)
- **Tuition**: 100% Free.
- **Focus Areas**: High-tech certifications including AI Robotics, AI (ML/DL), Web & Mobile Application Development, Cyber Security, and Cloud Computing.

### C. Akhuwat & GIZ Sponsored Trainings
- Subsidized and free vocational skill development tracks (e.g. Amazon Virtual Assistant, Graphic Design, and Cloud tracks).
